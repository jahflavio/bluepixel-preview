Information Security Management System Policy

At Bluepixel Strategic Innovation S.A. de C.V., there is a firm commitment to the protection and safeguarding of information throughout all stages of its processes, including the analysis, design, development, implementation, operation, maintenance, and support of digital platforms and technological solutions. The organization ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information, as well as compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

To this end, Bluepixel establishes, implements, and maintains an Information Security Management System (ISMS) based on a risk management and continuous improvement approach, aimed at providing secure, reliable, and high-value services, strengthening the trust of customers, suppliers, and business partners, and fostering an organizational culture of information security at all levels of the company.

Agentic engineering, cloud-native development and the FutureProof guarantee for companies in Mexico and Latin America.

Current CyberVadis certification.
