Frequently asked questions

About IMPATH™ and FutureProof™

What is IMPATH™?

It is BluePixel’s proprietary methodology for understanding user behavior and detecting the friction that costs an operation the most. It is delivered as an operational friction diagnostic within Digital Consulting and serves as the basis for design in the other ways of working.

How do IMPATH™ and FutureProof™ relate?

IMPATH™ is the method for finding the friction that costs the most; FutureProof™ is the guarantee of how whatever comes out of that diagnostic is built. The five principles are on the FutureProof page.

What do I receive from an operational friction diagnostic?

An IMPATH™ operational friction diagnostic, the recommended technical architecture, a backlog prioritized by financial impact, a business case with projected ROI and the cost of inaction calculation.
