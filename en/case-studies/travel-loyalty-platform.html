Latin American airline case study

One sign-in, miles in plain sight and benefits on visual cards.

Specialties: UX · UI · Web development · Responsive product · Loyalty platforms

screen to sign in and register

Project facts as stated in its published case sheet. Result figures are published only with a source and a date.

The goal

Where we started

Redesign the loyalty program section to improve usability, modernize the interface and streamline sign-in and registration, so managing miles, benefits and the account would be clearer.

The challenge

Friction across the key journeys of the loyalty platform, from sign-in to checking benefits.

  • Fragmented sign-in and registration
  • Unclear information architecture
  • Account data and benefits hard to scan
  • An experience that did not reflect a modern travel brand
The approach

How we did it

  1. 01 Requirements analysis
  2. 02 Research and insight extraction
  3. 03 Information architecture restructuring
  4. 04 Wireframing
  5. 05 UI discovery and visual design
The solution

Compare the flow: before and after

Access on a single screen, with information on one side and context imagery on the other; an account built around miles; benefits on visual cards.

Impact

What changed

  • A more intuitive and accessible loyalty platform
  • Clearer navigation and content hierarchy
  • Faster sign-in and account management
