The goal

Where we started

Design an intuitive, trustworthy platform to rent premium suites and seats and buy tickets safely, with a booking flow that balances trust, speed and clarity for buyers and owners.

The challenge

One product for two audiences: people looking for tickets and premium suites, and owners who want to rent theirs out securely.

  • Slow event search
  • Unclear seat and suite selection
  • Low trust during purchase
  • Event details lost during booking
