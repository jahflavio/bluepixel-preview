About us

A consultancy that holds itself to its own rules

Design and engineering on one team. Five rules, each with its proof.

  1. Principle 1

    Anti-commodity, not a body shop

    Scope and investment defined before work starts, not blind hourly billing.

  2. Principle 2

    Certainty first: validate before coding

    Cost of inaction, inference and hosting calculated before you invest.

  3. Principle 3

    UX as armor and multiplier

    Before any code, the prototype is tested with real users.

  4. Principle 4

    Decoupled architecture and data sovereignty

    Your code and data in your cloud; switching models is configuration.

  5. Principle 5

    Enterprise-grade security

    Zero-Trust and OWASP Top 10 from the first sprint. Current CyberVadis certification.
