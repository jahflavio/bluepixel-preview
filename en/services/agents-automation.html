Agents & Automation: AI agents on your ERP in 2 to 4 weeks

Without replacing your stack. We automate on top of what already works.

For companies that want to connect AI agents and automations to their current systems without replacing them.

Book a meeting Message us on WhatsApp
The problem

Why AI never reaches production

  1. 01

    Assistants that only answer

    They answer common questions, but cannot check a balance, reconcile an invoice or update the ERP.

  2. 02

    Fear that it makes things up or leaks data

    No one wants AI answering a customer without support, or confidential information ending up in a public model.

  3. 03

    Systems that do not talk to each other

    People copying data between screens because the AI lives apart from the systems that run the operation.

Week by week

What the path looks like

  1. 01

    Week 1 · Mapping and connectors

    We define the process rules, the systems it touches and how the knowledge base is built.

    You get: Flow map · MCP connector specification

  2. 02

    Week 2 · Agent in testing

    We load company data, build the agent’s tools and test it in a separate environment.

    You get: Agent in a test environment

  3. 03

    Week 3 · Guardrails

    We test edge cases, tune the security filters and define which actions a person approves.

    You get: Accuracy and security report

  4. 04

    Week 4 · Production

    We deploy it in your private cloud, train the team and switch on telemetry.

    You get: Agent in production · Live telemetry

This is what a 4-week agent looks like. A simple flow with a single system can take 2; the exact plan is set when we agree on it.

What you take away

Deliverables

  • Agent in production

    In your private cloud, running real tasks of the process.

  • MCP connectors

    Connections to your ERP or CRM with documented contracts. Switching models becomes a configuration change.

  • Private RAG

    It answers with your documents and data, cites the source and abstains when it has no support.

  • Oversight console

    Your team reviews, approves and audits what the agent does.

  • Security guardrails

    Filters against prompt injection and masking of personal data.

  • Telemetry

    How many tasks the agent resolves, how long it takes and where it needs a person.

Before you decide

Frequently asked questions

What do we need ready to start?

A process with rules that can be described and access to the systems it touches. The self-assessment on this page tells you how close you are.

Who from our team takes part?

Whoever knows the process and whoever runs the systems it touches: they define the rules at the start and approve the tests before production.

Do agents replace our team?

No. They take the repetitive work off your team so it can supervise and focus on what needs judgment.

What happens after week 4?

The agent keeps running in your cloud and your team supervises it. To add agents or tune the ones already running, there is Digital Evolution (Service 04).
