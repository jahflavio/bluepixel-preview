Week by week

What the path looks like

  1. 01

    Week 1 · Immersion

    We learn the business and the operation with key leaders and review the systems already in place.

    You get: Friction map · Stack inventory

  2. 02

    Week 2 · Audit

    We review code, APIs and data, and test the critical flows with users.

    You get: IMPATH™ diagnosis · Technical debt status

  3. 03

    Week 3 · Architecture

    We design the recommended architecture and calculate cloud and inference costs.

    You get: Recommended technical architecture · Cost model

  4. 04

    Week 4 · Business case

    We prioritize the backlog by impact and present it to leadership with the projected return.

    You get: Business case with ROI · Prioritized backlog

This is what a 4-week engagement looks like. A smaller scope takes 2 weeks; the exact plan is set when we agree on it.

Before you decide

Frequently asked questions

What if we decide not to build afterwards?

You avoided investing in something that would not pay off. The deliverables are yours to use whenever you decide.

Do we have to hire BluePixel for the development?

No. The four services are independent. The architecture and backlog are delivered on open standards so any team, internal or external, can execute them.

How much of our team’s time does it take?

A few working sessions with key leaders. We do the technical analysis and modeling. The number of sessions is set when we agree on the scope.

How much does the consulting cost?

The investment depends on the scope and is set before we start. You can estimate your cost of inaction with the calculator on the Consulting capability page.
