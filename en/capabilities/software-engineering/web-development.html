Decide

Static site, self-managed site, online store or custom application?

What it must do, who will update it, what it integrates with and how custom your logic is: from that, you see the option and the rule that picked it. Guidance, not a quote.

Self-managed site with a content management system

If the marketing team publishes and edits without depending on development, a content management system gives it autonomy over text and images while design and components stay under control.

rule applied     = inform + marketing edits → self-managed site
1. portal with sign-in or own logic      → custom application
2. sell                                  → online store
3. inform + marketing edits              → self-managed site
4. inform + changes through development  → static site

These are general engineering criteria to guide you, not a proposal or a quote: the final decision depends on the case, the team that will maintain the site and its roadmap.

Frequently asked questions

About web development

What kind of websites and web platforms do you develop?

Corporate websites, online stores, web applications and portals for companies. Each one is designed and tested with users before we write the code.

Can we update the content ourselves?

It depends on who will maintain it. If your marketing team must publish without asking development for changes, it is built with a content management system; if changes go through the technical team, a static site is faster and simpler to protect. The tool on this page guides you.

Do you develop online stores?

Yes: catalog, product pages and checkout, with the integrations to your ERP or payment gateways that are needed. You can compare the before and after of the mobile shopping redesign for an electronics retailer.

How long does it take to develop a web platform?

An online store, a portal or a web application falls under Digital Platforms: 2 to 4 months to production. The scope of each site is set in the technical estimate.

How much does it cost to develop a website or web platform?

It depends on the type of site and how many integrations it needs, so there is no list price. Before you invest, you get a technical architecture estimate. If you need the business case with ROI and the cost of inaction, Digital Consulting delivers it in 2 to 4 weeks.

Does the site connect to our ERP or CRM?

Yes. The site reads from and updates your ERP or CRM, and those systems remain the source of truth.

How does the site keep improving its conversion?

The site ships with automated tests and production deployment with SLA 99.9%. If you want someone to keep improving its conversion, Digital Evolution assigns a squad with a living roadmap of 6 or 12 months.
