What is a decoupled architecture?

A decoupled architecture splits the system into independent pieces that talk through stable interfaces, so one can be changed or scaled without breaking the others. It is what lets you switch cloud provider or AI model by configuration.

A software development company that is measured in production

Every platform ships with automated tests, a deployment pipeline and a decoupled architecture: swapping a vendor or a model is a configuration change, not a rebuild.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Software Engineering

What technologies do you use?

React, Node.js, Python, TypeScript and PostgreSQL, on serverless or container architectures on AWS, GCP or Azure, with automated CI/CD pipelines. The final choice depends on your current stack.

Who owns the code?

Your company: the repositories and infrastructure live in your cloud account, with a decoupled architecture and open protocols. Ownership terms are set in each project’s contract.

Do you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. We can join your team as one more cell or deliver complete modules ready for production, with the code and documentation in your repositories.
