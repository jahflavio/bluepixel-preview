What is an AI agent?

A system that receives a goal, decides the steps and uses tools (querying a system, calculating, sending a request) to get it done, under defined supervision and limits. An assistant talks; an agent acts. For example: it resolves a customer request end to end, reconciles invoices against the ERP or validates the documents to onboard a supplier.

What is RAG?

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) is the technique in which the model first searches for relevant passages in your own knowledge base and answers based on them, citing the source. It reduces made-up answers because the agent can abstain when it finds no supporting evidence.

How is it different from RPA?

RPA automates fixed, predictable steps; an agent handles exceptions and natural language. In real projects they are often combined: RPA for the repetitive and agents for what requires judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Agentic AI & Automation

Is my data sent to a public model?

We design so that data and code reside in your private cloud, and the model is configurable to avoid dependence on a single vendor.

Do you replace my ERP or my CRM?

No. We connect the agents to your current systems (SAP, Salesforce, ERPs, APIs) through the MCP protocol, without replacing them.

Which AI models do you work with?

Whichever solves your case best. The architecture is model-agnostic: switching models or providers is configuration, not rebuilding the agent.
