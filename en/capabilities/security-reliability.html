What is Zero-Trust architecture?

Zero-Trust is a security model in which no user, service or network is trusted by default: every access is verified and limited to the minimum needed. It reduces the possible damage if a credential or a component is exposed.

Technical sovereignty

Your infrastructure. Your data. Your source code.

It is the fourth principle of the FutureProof guarantee: decoupled architecture and data sovereignty.

Your infrastructure

We deploy inside your company's private cloud (VPC), with network isolation and encryption at rest and in transit.

Your data

Your corporate data resides in your private cloud. Agents and RAG work on it inside that perimeter, with guardrails and DLP for AI.

Your source code

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Security & Reliability

What security credentials do you hold?

We hold a current CyberVadis certification and apply OWASP Top 10 best practices in every release.

What exactly does the security capability cover?

The security of what we build: Zero-Trust architecture, OWASP Top 10 hardening in every release, code and dependency analysis in the pipeline, encryption and VPC isolation, and guardrails for AI. The independent audit is done by a specialized firm; we fix what it finds.

Can you remediate the findings of an external audit?

Yes. We receive the findings delivered by a security firm and remediate them in the code and in the architecture.

Can you help me answer a vendor's security questionnaire?

On the technical side, yes. We deliver documented technical controls (encryption, VPC isolation, access traceability, code and dependency analysis in the pipeline) that serve as a basis for answering the vendor questionnaires of your customer or your procurement team.

Where does my data live?

In your own private cloud (VPC), with encryption at rest and in transit and access traceability.

Can I change cloud provider or AI model later?

Yes. We design decoupled architectures on open protocols such as MCP, with no vendor lock-in: if a new model comes out or you change provider, the system switches through configuration without rebuilding the platform. The code and data reside in your private cloud.

How is the data an AI agent uses protected?

With guardrails and DLP for AI, protection of prompts and RAG data, isolation in your VPC, and encryption at rest and in transit.
