What is product telemetry?

Product telemetry is the record of what users do inside a platform (what they open, where they pause, where they drop off), with event names defined in advance. It turns assumptions into evidence when deciding what to improve first.

What is predictive analytics?

Analytics has three steps: descriptive (what happened), diagnostic (why it happened) and predictive (what will happen). Predictive analytics uses historical and behavioral data to anticipate, for example, which customers are about to leave, and act before it happens.
