What is a Design System?

A Design System is the set of components, rules and design tokens shared by design and engineering: a button is defined once and used the same way across the whole product. It cuts rework and keeps the interface consistent as the product grows.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about UX/UI & Product Strategy

What is the difference between UX/UI and Product Strategy?

UX/UI defines how the product looks and feels; Product Strategy defines which product to build, for whom and in what order. We work on them together so that the design answers to a measurable business goal.

Do you validate the design with real users?

Yes. In the Digital Platforms service, the prototype is validated with real users before moving to development.

Do you deliver a Design System?

Yes. We deliver documented components and tokens that the engineering team consumes directly.

Do you also build the code?

Yes. Software Engineering builds what is designed, or we hand over the documented Design System and prototype for your team to build.

Can you redesign a platform that is already in production?

Yes. We first map where users get stuck and redesign what has the most impact, in stages and without stopping operations.
